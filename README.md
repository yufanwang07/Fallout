# Fallout Legendary Weapons

This is a work-in-progress standalone legendary weapons pack for Fallout SMP, inspired by the legendary weapons from Altar SMP and Hoplite.

## Features

- **Legendary weapon framework**: Adds custom legendary netherite swords with loot tables, custom data markers, advancements, tick hooks, and utility give functions.
- **Pale Blade**: A combo-focused legendary sword with sweeping slash patterns, miss and crit variants, short target glow, custom particles, sounds, and hit sensors.
- **The Descent**: A fall-powered legendary sword that turns aerial hits into smash impacts with AOE damage, main-target bonus damage, rebound teleporting, short blindness, darkness, resistance, and custom impact particles.
- **Dragon Egg flow**: Tracks dragon egg ownership and reform timing, including death handling, warning titles, item return, and temporary boon effects.
- **Shared effects and debug tools**: Includes reusable particle and effect functions plus debug helpers for testing reform timers and legendary weapon behavior.

## Resource Pack

Weapon models and textures live separately on the `feature/resource-pack` branch as a resource-pack zip.
